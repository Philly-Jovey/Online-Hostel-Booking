Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about booking student hostels, payments, and campus security.

When is the best time to book my hostel for the upcoming semester? expand_more
We recommend booking 3 to 5 weeks before the university reporting date. High-demand self-contained single rooms near Makerere Kikoni, MUBS Nakawa, and Kyambogo East Gate fill up very quickly. Early booking ensures you get your preferred floor and room orientation.
Can I pay rent in installments through Mobile Money? expand_more
Yes! Most partner hostels on Online Hostel Booking allow an initial booking deposit (typically 30% to 50%) via MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money to lock your room. The remaining balance can be scheduled in agreed installments directly with the hostel custodian before mid-semester exams.
How does Online Hostel Booking protect me from fake hostel brokers? expand_more
Unlike informal street brokers who charge viewing fees and frequently vanish, every hostel on Online Hostel Booking is verified in person by our physical field team. Your payment is held safely until you receive your room key, and all bookings generate a verifiable digital receipt acknowledged by the registered hostel owner.
What happens if I need to cancel or change rooms? expand_more
If your intake status changes or you need to shift to another hostel wing, Online Hostel Booking provides a 72-hour cancellation grace period from booking time. Our student support team assists in reallocating your deposit without penalty.
